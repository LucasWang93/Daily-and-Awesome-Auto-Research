## AutoLLMResearch: Training Research Agents for Automating LLM Experiment Configuration -- Learning from Cheap, Optimizing Expensive

- ID: `2605.11518`
- Score: 9.55
- Themes: End-to-End AI Scientists
- Link: https://huggingface.co/papers/2605.11518

AutoLLMResearch introduces a novel framework for automating the configuration of large language model (LLM) experiments by learning from low-cost trials to optimize high-cost setups. It features LLMConfig-Gym, a multi-fidelity environment backed by over one million GPU hours, and uses a Markov Decision Process for efficient decision-making. This approach shows promise in reducing the labor and resources required for scalable LLM research.

**Why it matters today:** With the rising costs of training large language models, tools like AutoLLMResearch are increasingly critical for sustainable AI development.


## AgentDisCo: Towards Disentanglement and Collaboration in Open-ended Deep Research Agents

AgentDisCo is a novel AI framework that disentangles exploration and exploitation in deep research workflows using collaborative agents. It introduces GALA, a benchmark for personalized research recommendations derived from user browsing history, and delivers visually rich research outputs. Evaluated on multiple benchmarks, it achieves competitive or superior performance, showcasing its potential to revolutionize automated research processes.

**Why it matters today:** As AI-driven research tools gain traction, AgentDisCo's focus on personalization and collaboration addresses critical gaps in existing systems, making it highly relevant today.

## Deep Reasoning in General Purpose Agents via Structured Meta-Cognition

- ID: `2605.11388v1`

This innovation reduces errors like hallucinations and premature reasoning, marking a step forward in flexible, general-purpose AI systems.

**Why it matters today:** This research is timely as adaptive reasoning is crucial for advancing AI's reliability in real-world applications.


## Rethinking Agentic Search with Pi-Serini: Is Lexical Retrieval Sufficient?

- ID: `2605.10848v1`
- Score: 7.55
- Themes: Literature And Survey Agents
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10848v1

Pi-Serini pairs the BM25 lexical retriever with advanced LLMs to explore whether lexical retrieval suffices in deep research tasks. Achieving 83.1% answer accuracy and 94.7% evidence recall, it outperforms dense retriever-based agents on BrowseComp-Plus. The study underscores the potential of optimized lexical retrieval for cost-efficient, high-performance research systems.

**Why it matters today:** This research is timely as it challenges the trend toward dense retrieval methods, offering a simpler yet effective alternative.
