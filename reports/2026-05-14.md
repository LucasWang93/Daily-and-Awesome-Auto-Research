## ViDR: Grounding Multimodal Deep Research Reports in Source Visual Evidence

- ID: `2605.13034v1`
- Score: 7.55
- Themes: Literature And Survey Agents
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13034v1

ViDR is a new multimodal framework that improves the grounding of research reports by integrating source visual evidence, such as figures and charts, alongside textual data. It refines noisy images, validates visual references, and links claims to evidence for better report quality and verifiability. The framework also introduces MMR Bench+, a benchmark for evaluating visual evidence use in research systems.

**Why it matters today:** As AI-generated research becomes more prevalent, frameworks like ViDR are essential for ensuring scientific rigor and trustworthiness.


## PresentAgent-2: Towards Generalist Multimodal Presentation Agents

- ID: `2605.11363v1`
- Score: 7.55
- Themes: Literature And Survey Agents
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11363v1

PresentAgent-2 is a cutting-edge framework for generating research-grounded, multimodal presentation videos from user queries. It supports three modes—Single Presentation, Discussion, and Interaction—while integrating text, images, GIFs, and videos. By moving beyond static slide creation, it enables dynamic and interactive presentations tailored to user needs.

**Why it matters today:** This tool is especially relevant as demand for engaging, AI-driven content creation continues to grow.
