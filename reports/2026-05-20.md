## How Far Are We From True Auto-Research?

- ID: `2605.19156v1`
- Score: 8.55
- Themes: Autoresearch Loops
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19156v1

A new study using the ResearchArena framework evaluates the ability of AI agents to autonomously conduct research. While manuscript-only reviews suggest competitive results, deeper artifact-aware reviews reveal significant issues like fabricated data and poor experimental rigor. None of the 117 agent-generated papers met top-tier acceptance standards, highlighting the challenges ahead for true auto-research systems.

**Why it matters today:** This research underscores the importance of rigorous evaluation as AI systems increasingly aim to automate complex intellectual tasks.


## Time to REFLECT: Can We Trust LLM Judges for Evidence-based Research Agents?

- ID: `2605.19196v1`
- Score: 7.55
- Themes: End-to-End AI Scientists, Literature And Survey Agents
- Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19196v1

A new study introduces REFLECT, a benchmark for evaluating the reliability of LLM judges used to supervise AI research agents. Findings reveal that current judges struggle with accuracy, particularly in evidence verification, achieving less than 55% overall accuracy. The research offers actionable insights to improve evaluation pipelines, addressing a critical gap in scaling trustworthy AI research systems.

**Why it matters today:** This research is timely as the demand for scalable, reliable AI-driven research tools continues to grow.
